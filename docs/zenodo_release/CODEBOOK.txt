OVERVIEW
--------
This dataset gives parcel-level estimates of the land area usable for
ground-mount solar PV and land-based wind at U.S. manufacturing parcels in
the contiguous United States (CONUS). It compares two estimators:

  1. The NLCD land-cover-proportion method. Usable area is the sum over
     NLCD 2021 classes of the class area in the parcel times a
     class-specific availability percentage.
  2. The reV composite exclusion method. Usable area is the parcel area
     times the mean inclusion fraction of the NLR reV composite exclusion
     raster cells whose centres fall inside the parcel. Three siting
     scenarios are given (Open, Reference, Limited). Wind is given for up
     to five turbine configurations.

The parcel universe is built from M-FIND, the Manufacturing Facility
Inventory National Dataset, version 1.1 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.21827821).
One parcel can host several M-FIND facilities. Rows are parcels, not
facilities.

Parcel boundaries come from LightBox SmartParcels (licensed) and are NOT
redistributed. The licensed LightBox parcel identifier is not included.
Rows are keyed to public M-FIND v1.1 obs_id values instead. The file holds
no coordinates. Join to M-FIND on obs_id for facility location and NAICS
attributes.


IDENTIFIERS AND PARCEL ATTRIBUTES
---------------------------------

 1. primary_obs_id            [string, 10 characters]
    Primary key. The alphabetically first M-FIND v1.1 obs_id among the
    facilities on this parcel. The choice of the first ID is arbitrary.
    It has no meaning beyond giving each parcel a stable key.

 2. mfind_obs_ids             [string]
    All M-FIND v1.1 obs_ids on this parcel, sorted and separated by
    semicolons (e.g. 0a1b2c3d4e;9z8y7x6w5v). Each obs_id appears in
    exactly one row. To get facility-level rows, split on ";" and join to
    M-FIND on obs_id.

 3. n_mfind_facilities        [integer]
    Number of M-FIND v1.1 facilities on the parcel. Ranges from 1 to 42.
    27,274 parcels host more than one facility.

 4. mfind_colocation_group_id [integer]
    The M-FIND v1.1 colocation_group_id shared by the facilities on this
    parcel. It is empty when the parcel hosts one facility. M-FIND
    assigns these IDs per release, so they are valid against v1.1 only.
    Use obs_id for joins to other M-FIND versions.

 5. county_fips               [string, 5 digits]
    County FIPS code (state + county) of the primary facility, from
    M-FIND FIPS_CODE, zero-padded. Read it as text to keep leading zeros.

 6. state                     [string, 2 letters]
    USPS code of the state batch the parcel was processed in. This is the
    state used for state results in the paper. It matches the state
    prefix of county_fips for all but two parcels on State Line Road
    (one on the NY/NJ line, one on the MO/KS line). M-FIND STATE is blank
    for about 18,700 of the primary facilities, so it is not used.

 7. parcel_acres              [numeric, acres]
    Parcel area: M-FIND PARCEL_AREA_SQFT / 43,560. All usable-area
    values below are fractions of this area.

 8. size_bin                  [string]
    Parcel-size class. Each bin includes its lower bound and excludes its
    upper bound:
      tiny    < 0.5 ac          (58,272 parcels)
      small   0.5 to < 1 ac     (35,160)
      medium  1 to < 10 ac      (142,875)
      large   10 to < 100 ac    (57,129)
      xlarge  >= 100 ac         (11,687)


NLCD LAND-COVER COMPOSITION
---------------------------
Source: NLCD 2021 Land Cover (Dewitz 2023, doi:10.5066/P9JZ7AO3), 30 m.
Class areas are fractional zonal sums computed in Google Earth Engine.
These columns let users re-run the NLCD method with their own
availability percentages.

 9–24. nlcd_CC_ac             [numeric, acres]
    Area of NLCD class CC within the parcel, for the 16 CONUS classes:
      11 Open Water                    41 Deciduous Forest
      12 Perennial Ice/Snow            42 Evergreen Forest
      21 Developed, Open Space         43 Mixed Forest
      22 Developed, Low Intensity      52 Shrub/Scrub
      23 Developed, Medium Intensity   71 Grassland/Herbaceous
      24 Developed, High Intensity     81 Pasture/Hay
      31 Barren Land                   82 Cultivated Crops
                                       90 Woody Wetlands
                                       95 Emergent Herbaceous Wetlands

25. nlcd_coverage_ratio       [numeric, ratio]
    Sum of the NLCD class areas divided by the parcel area. It is close
    to 1 for most parcels. Values above 1 arise from fractional cell
    weighting at parcel edges. For this reason solar_nlcd_ac exceeds
    parcel_acres on 2 parcels and wind_nlcd_pct_only_ac on 1.

26. qa_nlcd_no_value          [boolean]
    True if the parcel had no NLCD return because of an invalid source
    geometry (3 parcels). Its NLCD class areas and NLCD usable areas
    are 0.


SOLAR USABLE AREA
-----------------

27. solar_nlcd_ac             [numeric, acres]
    NLCD-method solar usable area at the baseline availability
    percentages (paper Table 1):
      Barren 80; Shrub/Scrub 70; Grassland 70; Pasture/Hay 60;
      Cultivated Crops 60; Developed Open Space 30; Developed Low 30;
      Developed Medium 20; Developed High 20; Deciduous Forest 10;
      Mixed Forest 15; Evergreen Forest 5; Open Water 0;
      Perennial Ice/Snow 0; Woody and Emergent Wetlands 0  (percent).

28–31. Forest-availability sensitivity (NLCD solar). These match
    solar_nlcd_ac except for the three forest classes (41 deciduous /
    42 evergreen / 43 mixed):
    solar_nlcd_forest_zero_ac        0 / 0 / 0 %
    solar_nlcd_forest_uniform10_ac  10 / 10 / 10 %
    solar_nlcd_forest_uniform50_ac  50 / 50 / 50 %  (empirical upper
                                     bound from Virginia DEQ project
                                     footprints; not a normative value)
    solar_nlcd_forest_doubled_ac    20 / 10 / 30 %  (2x baseline)
    The baseline (10 / 5 / 15 %) is solar_nlcd_ac.

42. solar_rev_open_ac         [numeric, acres]
43. solar_rev_reference_ac    [numeric, acres]
44. solar_rev_limited_ac      [numeric, acres]
    reV-method solar usable area under the NLR 2024 supply-curve Open,
    Reference and Limited siting scenarios (Lopez et al. 2025,
    doi:10.2172/2500362). Computed from the 90 m solar composite
    exclusion rasters released on OEDI (submission 8319,
    https://data.openei.org/submissions/8319).
    TODO: update the OEDI file names and DOI once NLR posts the 90 m
    composites (planned to replace the 92.11 m files now listed).

    reV Parcel Joined (revPJ) is NOT included per parcel. Its raster is
    built on the licensed LightBox parcel fabric. Totals by size bin and
    by state are in the summary files (column
    solar_rev_parcel_joined_ac).


WIND USABLE AREA
----------------

32. wind_nlcd_pct_only_ac     [numeric, acres]
    NLCD-method wind usable area at the baseline wind availability
    percentages (paper Table 1):
      Barren 80; Shrub/Scrub 70; Grassland 70; Pasture/Hay 70;
      Cultivated Crops 70; Developed Open Space 20; Developed Low 10;
      Developed Medium 5; Developed High 5; Deciduous Forest 10;
      Mixed Forest 10; Evergreen Forest 5; Water, Ice/Snow, Wetlands 0
      (percent).

33–39. Binary parcel exclusion flags for the NLCD wind method
    [boolean; True = parcel meets the exclusion condition]
    flag_lt_1ac                  parcel_acres < 1                (93,432)
    flag_urban                   intersects a TIGER/Line 2023
                                 Urban Area polygon (UACE20)     (229,624)
    flag_dod_sua                 intersects FAA Special Use
                                 Airspace flagged DoD restricted (13,122)
    flag_usfws_critical_habitat  intersects a USFWS Critical
                                 Habitat polygon                 (4,083)
    flag_airport_3km             within 3 km of an FAA airport
                                 facility centroid               (131,561)
    flag_slope_gt25              any overlapping 3DEP 1-arc-second
                                 cell with slope >= 25 %         (11,852)
    flag_nwi                     intersects a USFWS National
                                 Wetlands Inventory polygon      (71,513)

40. wind_nlcd_5layer_ac       [numeric, acres]
    wind_nlcd_pct_only_ac, set to 0 if any of the first five flags
    (lt_1ac, urban, dod_sua, usfws_critical_habitat, airport_3km) is
    True. This is the headline NLCD wind estimate.

41. wind_nlcd_7layer_ac       [numeric, acres]
    wind_nlcd_pct_only_ac, set to 0 if any of the seven flags is True.
    Diagnostic only. See the paper's NWI coverage-fraction diagnostic.

45–52. reV-method wind usable area [numeric, acres]
    Computed from NLR reV wind composite exclusion rasters on a common
    90 m grid (2024 supply-curve edition). Turbines are given as hub
    height (hh, m) / rotor diameter (rd, m):
    wind_rev_open_ac                    Open (no turbine-specific
                                        setback; common to all turbines)
    wind_rev_reference_hh115_rd170_ac   Reference, 115/170 (utility scale)
    wind_rev_limited_hh115_rd170_ac     Limited,   115/170
    wind_rev_reference_hh80_rd77_ac     Reference, 80/77 (1.5 MW class)
    wind_rev_limited_hh80_rd77_ac       Limited,   80/77
    wind_rev_reference_hh60_rd47_ac     Reference, 60/47
    wind_rev_reference_hh37_rd20p7_ac   Reference, 37/20.7
    wind_rev_reference_hh24_rd9p6_ac    Reference, 24/9.6
    The 115/170 layers were regridded by NLR from their native ~92.1 m
    grid. NLR produced the other configurations for this study.

reV QUALITY-ASSURANCE FLAGS
---------------------------
reV usable area = parcel_acres x mean inclusion fraction of the cells
whose centres fall inside the parcel. QA flags are identical across
scenarios on the same grid, so one set is given per technology.

53. qa_rev_solar_centroid_fallback  [boolean]
55. qa_rev_wind_centroid_fallback   [boolean]
    True if the inclusion fraction was taken from the single cell that
    contains the parcel centroid. This applies to all parcels under
    1 acre and to any larger parcel with no cell centre inside it.

54. qa_rev_solar_low_pixel_count    [boolean]
56. qa_rev_wind_low_pixel_count     [boolean]
    True if fewer than 3 cell centres fall inside the parcel
    (centroid-fallback parcels are also True). Estimates for these
    parcels rest on very few 90 m cells.

No parcel lacked a reV value (flag_rev_no_value was 0 everywhere and is
omitted).

NOTES ON DATA HANDLING
----------------------
- Read county_fips and primary_obs_id as text, e.g.
  pd.read_csv(f, dtype={"county_fips": str, "primary_obs_id": str}).
- Facility-level use. Usable area belongs to the parcel. Do not sum it
  over facilities from M-FIND; that double-counts shared parcels. To
  attach parcel values to facilities, split mfind_obs_ids and copy the
  parcel value to each facility.
- Technical potential only. Values are land-availability estimates. They
  do not account for resource quality, interconnection, ownership,
  operations on the site, or developer intent.
- M-FIND v1.1 contains 87 CONUS facility records that are not in this
  parcel universe. The universe was fixed on 2026-04-23, before those
  records were added. See paper Section 2.1.
